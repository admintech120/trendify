RETURN & EXCHANGE PROCEDURE

YOU CAN APPLY FOR A RETURN OR EXCHANGE WITHIN 7 DAYS

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, please return it to us within 7 days of your purchase, and we will gladly issue a replacement or store credit within a week of receiving the returned goods.

Items must be unworn, unwashed, undamaged and unused with all original tags attached for a return to be acceptable.

• BUYER PAYS THE RETURN COURIER CHARGES.

• ALTERED OR DAMAGED ITEMS ARE NOT ELIGIBLE FOR RETURN OR EXCHANGE.

RETURN & EXCHANGE STEPS
  1. TAKE A PICTURE OF THE DEFECTIVE PART OF THE ITEM
  2. PACK THE ITEM BACK IN THE ORIGINAL PACKAGING
  3. EMAIL US [fajarfateh@gmail.com] WITH PICTURES AND ORDER ID
  4. WE WILL PROVIDE YOU THE RETURN COURIER ADDRESS
  5. DISPATCH VIA LOCAL COURIER AND SEND US THE TRACKING NUMBER
